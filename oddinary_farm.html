Overview

Oddinary Farm is a arcade-style co-op farming game built in Unity. The game combines classic farming mechanics with chaotic gameplay by juggling casual building with a tower defense aspect. The game is made by Spring Rolls Studio, a small team made up of college graduates. The lead programmer is Khoa Ngyuen and associate lead programmer is me, Ryan Carpenter. The game hosts peer-to-peer multiplayer utilizing Steamworks Facepunch and Unity's Netcode for Gameobjects. Open playtest is out now, link above!

Gameplay

Oddinary Farm is a top-down survival game playable solo or in online co-op, combining cozy farming with high-risk exploration and combat. Think Stardew Valley meets Terraria. Players build and defend a vulnerable farm, explore a resource-rich island by day, and survive alien attacks by night. The goal is to gather resources and prepare a hot air balloon to escape within 6 to 10 days, or face inevitable doom.

Implemented Features

Name Description
Peer-to-peer Multiplayer Unity Netcode for GameObjects, transported over Facepunch (Steamworks). Networking is deeply woven through the codebase, not confined to one layer. Allows player clients to connect to a host over the internet through steam invites.
Procedural Generation The system builds a synchronized island-world out of four layered Perlin noise maps (elevation, moisture, ore, and resource), then streams terrain/foliage/resources in and out around each player in real time. Map generation is done client-side with a seed to improve performance.
Inventory System Dynamic and scalable slot-based inventory system akin to Minecraft's system. Items are data-driven and situational. The player can perform a variety of actions depending on the item including placement, combat, and buffs.
Object Pooling Game reuses identical item/objects instead of creating them to improve performance and frame rate.
Mounts Player can mount animals and other entities to increase movement speed and other properties.
Economy Shop, wallet/currency, quotas, loot tables/generator, and gambling.
Day and Night Cycle Time scale that affects the day/night cycle of the game. This changes the state of the game and the events that happen.
