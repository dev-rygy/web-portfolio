overview: |
  *Bloodlust* is a game I created with a colleague for [Ludum Dare Game Jam #49](https://ldjam.com/events/ludum-dare/49). Ludum Dare is one of the largest game jams in the world, challenging participants to create a complete game in 72 hours based on a particular theme. The theme was "Prevent the Inevitable," and given the timing near Halloween, we designed a game about a vampire who must feast on victims to survive.

  The game features AI with A* pathfinding, a dynamic NPC scheduling system, a day and night cycle, and an inventory system. All assets and artwork are my own.

    - question: Primary Setbacks Before Release?
      answer: |
        One of the largest setbacks that came from building a legit title was scope creep. As stated from the previous section we wanted a full campaign from tutorial to the final boss (Satan), we wanted an endless mode for people who finished the campaigne for an incentive to continue playing, and we wanted a shop where players could purchase cosmetics with a secondary currency they would earn by completing checkpoints in both modes. All of this on top of polishing the final game dragged the development timeline on for around 5 months which severly trumped our original plan of just 4 weeks. 

    - question: Teamwork Insights
      answer: |
        The biggest thing I learned about working with teams on jams and many other projects is that people natually settle into roles as time goes by. This is especially true when features start to pile in the backlog. For this project in particular I decided to take on the roles of artist and animator. I made all the sprites, tiles, and sprite sheets. I animated them all using Unity's animator and dope sheet. Then I hooked them up in scripts where they needed to be.
