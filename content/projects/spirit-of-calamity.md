overview: |
  A restless spirit is a rare spirit that embodies the soul of its host. It is summoned after the host's demise only if the death was caused by great distress or some important duty was not fulfilled. Its origins remain unknown, but some say this spirit is a division of a *Spirit of Calamity* — a very powerful spirit capable of wielding the abilities of a demi-god. As a restless spirit, the player traverses a large castle where many untold horrors lie. Your goal is to find out why you were summoned and to carry out your destiny.

  *Spirit of Calamity* is perhaps the idea that really got me into game design. As a child I was fascinated with my ability to create intricate worlds and plots that even surprise me at times — and because in a video game you can create just that, and whatever else you can imagine, I never doubted that becoming a game designer was the career path for me. *Spirit of Calamity* is the birth of my creativity; it's a world I've fantasized about since I was a teenager and one I badly want to see come to life. This is the game I'm most passionate about, and I won't settle for less than ~~perfect~~ *nearly perfect*.
