This action-packed, lighthearted adventure spans three unique levels and two challenging boss fights. Players play as a former Border Guard of the Intragalactic Coalition as he becomes entangled in a rebellion to save the galaxy.

  As the club's vice president and systems lead, I oversaw project management, task organization, and project scheduling.

  - title: Objective System
    size: sub
    body: |
      Each level includes unique objectives such as defeating all enemies, surviving for a set time, or eliminating enemies within a time limit. The levels follow a linear progression based on this system.

  - title: Wave System
    size: sub
    body: |
      A dynamic wave system selects from a pool of unique waves for each level.

  - title: Player Abilities
    size: sub
    body: |
      Players use abilities like *Phase Shift* (temporary invulnerability) and *Pulse Bomb* (area damage), with cooldown timers and usage limits displayed on the HUD.

  - title: Player Arsenal
    size: sub
    body: |
      The player has access to a variety of weapons they can unlock throughout the game, including the *Pistol* (slow fire rate), the *Laser Gun* (charge for more damage and pierce through enemies), and the *Gatling Gun* (rapid fire with cooldown).

  - title: Boss Fights
    size: sub
    body: |
      The game features two challenging boss fights. The first boss requires destroying shield generators to disable his defenses while fending off minions. The second boss has a flurry of attacks, including a burst attack that sends out bullets in a fan, and a sweep attack where the boss goes back and forth continuously firing bullets rapidly.

  - title: Dialogue System
    size: sub
    body: |
      A full dialogue system with animated typewriter text and character portraits, along with eight cutscenes, immerses the player in the narrative.

  - title: Particle System and Shaders
    size: sub
    body: |
      Visual effects are inspired by classic arcade games, featuring Unity's Particle Pack and voxel-style effects made by members of the club.

  - title: Music and Sound
    size: sub
    body: |
      Original music and sound effects created by talented members of the club.
