PROJECTS

Labyrinth — Procedural Dungeon Generator

Nov 2024 - Present

The Labyrinth is an interconnected procedural dungeon generation algorithm built in Unity/C# that creates seamlessly joined, thematically distinct 3D areas without loading screens. Most procedural dungeon generators start from randomness and filter for playability. This one starts from intent. A designer authors a node graph describing rooms, roles, and connections. Those rules are converted into "Blueprints" and parsed later using recursive descent to generate the rooms.

Oddinary Farm — Multiplayer Farming Sim

May 2025 - July 2026

Oddinary Farm is a 2D arcade-style co-op farming game that was made in Unity/C# using Netcode For GameObjects and Steamworks Facepunch. Players can plant crops, build defenses, and explore a procedurally generated landscape. Created with a small team of college graduates, Spring Rolls Studios, the game has over 400+ wishlists and won Best Presentation, Kaedim Indie Showcase at GDC.
